================================================================================
                    SAFARGO PESHAWAR (سفر گو پشاور)
               COMPLETE PROJECT FEATURES & SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

Platform: Web, Mobile Responsive, Progressive Web App (PWA)
Target Region: Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan
Primary Tech Stack: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet Maps,
                    OSRM Road Routing, Supabase, Framer Motion

================================================================================
1. THREE-TIER SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The application is built with 3 integrated portals:
  [A] Passenger Portal (مسافر ایپ)
      - Instant ride & delivery booking, real-time map tracking, wallet,
        in-app chat/call, saved places, and SOS safety center.
  [B] Driver Portal (ڈرائیور پینل)
      - Online/offline availability switch, 30s incoming ride alert cards,
        turn-by-turn road navigation, trip controls, daily earnings & metrics.
  [C] Admin Management Portal (ایڈمن کنٹرول پینل)
      - Driver KYC document verification, payment screenshot review,
        1-click approve/reject, live rides dispatch monitor, and platform stats.

================================================================================
2. INTERACTIVE PESHAWAR MAP & ROAD NAVIGATION ENGINE
================================================================================
* Real Road-Following Routes (OSRM Engine):
  - Rides follow actual physical roads of Peshawar (GT Road, University Road,
    Ring Road, Jamrud Road, Khyber Road, Saddar) instead of straight lines.
* Google Maps Dual-Tone Visual Styling:
  - Outer dark-green casing line (8px) + Inner vibrant emerald-green line (5px).
* Smart Auto-Framing & Padding:
  - Automatically calculates bounds for Pickup and Dropoff locations with
    bottom-sheet padding so both pins are visible without manual zooming.
* Zoom Retention & Zero-Jitter Blue Dot:
  - Real GPS tracking updates only the user location Blue Dot without
    triggering unwanted map zoom-outs or reset-views.
  - Soft radar pulse effect without vibration or jitter.
* 4-in-1 Map Layer Switcher:
  1. CARTO Voyager (Clean, ultra-fast CDN vector tiles)
  2. Google Roads (Crisp road network)
  3. Satellite Hybrid (High-resolution aerial imagery)
  4. OpenStreetMap (Detailed community road map)
* Peshawar Landmark Hotspots:
  - Direct markers for Islamia College, KTH Hospital, Mall Road Saddar,
    Peshawar Museum, Tatara Park Phase 3, BRT Hayatabad, and Karkhano Market.
* Pin-on-Map Custom Selector:
  - Tap or drag anywhere on the map to place custom pins with reverse
    geocoding to the nearest Peshawar area.
* Vehicle Telemetry & Compass Bearing:
  - Driver marker rotates dynamically according to road heading bearing.
  - Live speed (km/h), remaining distance (km), and dynamic ETA (mins).

================================================================================
3. REAL-TIME RIDE MATCHING & BOOKING ENGINE
================================================================================
* Zero-Fake-Driver Architecture:
  - No fake accounts or bot simulations. Ride requests are delivered
    exclusively to genuine online drivers in real-time.
* Dynamic Travel Time & Distance Display:
  - Displays real road driving distance and duration on the booking sheet:
    Example: "12 mins • 6.4 km via Peshawar Roads".
* Supported Vehicle Categories:
  1. SafarGo Bike (موٹرسائیکل): Base PKR 60, Per KM PKR 22 (Fast solo travel)
  2. SafarGo Car AC (گاڑی): Base PKR 150, Per KM PKR 38 (AC family ride)
  3. SafarGo Cargo (لوڈر / پک اپ): Base PKR 280, Per KM PKR 55 (Goods/Luggage)
* Complete 6-Step Ride Lifecycle:
  1. 'searching'       -> Broadcast to online drivers in Peshawar
  2. 'driver_accepted' -> Locks passenger view to driver details & car info
  3. 'live_tracking'   -> Driver moves towards pickup location along road
  4. 'driver_arrived'  -> Audio chime & arrival notification to passenger
  5. 'ride_in_progress'-> OTP trip start; navigation routes to destination
  6. 'ride_completed'  -> Fare breakdown and 5-star rating dialog
* Built-in Audio Effects (Web Audio API):
  - Pop, click, success chime, driver arrival alert, and ride complete sounds.

================================================================================
4. DRIVER ONBOARDING, KYC & ADMIN APPROVAL LOCK
================================================================================
* 4-Step Registration Wizard:
  1. Personal Information (Name, Phone, CNIC, Verified DOB 18+, Emergency Contact)
  2. Vehicle Information (Category, Model, Registration Year, Number Plate, Color)
  3. KYC Document Uploads (CNIC Front/Back, License, Plate Photo, Selfie)
     - Includes integrated Image Cropper with canvas compression.
  4. Registration Fee Payment Verification (PKR 1,500)
     - EasyPaisa and JazzCash SafarGo Peshawar account display.
     - Upload receipt/payment screenshot.
* Strict Admin Approval Lock:
  - Submitted applications enter 'pending' status.
  - If user switches from passenger to driver before admin approval,
    the app strictly blocks the driver panel and shows the "Pending Review" screen.
  - Driver panel is unlocked ONLY when Admin clicks "Approve" in Admin Portal.

================================================================================
5. DRIVER PORTAL (DRIVER PANEL)
================================================================================
* Online / Offline Switch:
  - One-tap toggle broadcasting active presence to dispatch engine.
* Real-Time Incoming Ride Alerts:
  - 30-second countdown card with passenger name, pickup, dropoff, fare,
    and Accept/Decline action buttons with audio alert.
* Live Road Navigation View:
  - Road-following path with action buttons: "Arrived", "Start Trip", "Complete".
* Driver Earnings & Statistics Dashboard:
  - Today's earnings in PKR (net of commission).
  - Completed trips counter.
  - Customer rating score (out of 5.0).
  - Ride acceptance rate percentage.
